Aspirational Captial

To maintain the hopes and ambitions of a goal, through all physical and mental barriers. To dream about the possibilities in the future without real means of attaining the goal at current state.

Familial Capital

The cultural knowledges, cultivated by family members that carry a sense of community, history, memory, and cultural intuition. Familial capital can also reach out to “extended family,” who can include friends, dead families, and cousins. Familial capital can be fostered between different “families” through sports, school, and religious gatherings.

Social Capital

Networks of people and community who provide instrumental and emotional support to navigate through the hardships of society's institutions. Networks can help people attain their goals, such as higher education, job opportunities, and healthcare, as made prominent with People of Color, who use their social capital to attain their goals.
